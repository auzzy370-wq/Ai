Positioning

What it is / isn’t

Is — a proving ground

  • Full product & security challenges
  • Browser IDE + sandboxed execution
  • Evidence-based AI scoring
  • Architecture, incidents, team delivery

Isn’t — another quiz

  • Not LeetCode / trivia
  • Not take-home form theater
  • Not “trust the CV”
  • Not AI auto-hire without humans
