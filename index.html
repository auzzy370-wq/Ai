📱 Add to Home Screen (Required)

This app must be installed to your home screen to work like an app.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (box with arrow)
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner
  4. The app now appears as "MLBB Advisor" on your home screen

⚡ iPhone 16 Action Button — One-Press Access

Map your Action Button to open this app instantly while playing MLBB.

  1. Open Settings → Action Button
  2. Swipe to Shortcut
  3. Tap Choose a Shortcut
  4. Tap + Create Shortcut
  5. Add action: Open App → MLBB Advisor
  6. Save it, then select it in Action Button settings

Result: Press Action Button → advisor opens instantly. Press it again to go back to MLBB.

🔄 Fastest Workflow While Playing

  1. Press Action Button → advisor opens
  2. Tap your HP + enemy situation (2–3 taps)
  3. Read the advice card (2 seconds)
  4. Press Action Button again → back to MLBB
  5. Total away time: ~5 seconds

📋 Draft Phase Workflow

  1. Switch to Draft tab before your ranked game starts
  2. As bans/picks happen, tap each slot and enter the hero
  3. Read the advice card — it updates after every pick
  4. When it's your turn to pick, follow the recommended hero
